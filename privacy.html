Privacy policy

Your information, handled with care.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The Wellness Edit (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website or place an order, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, shipping address, phone number and messages you send through our contact form.
  • Order information: products purchased, order totals and payment confirmation. We never store full card numbers.
  • Usage information: pages visited, device type and approximate location, used to improve the site.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your orders.
  • To reply to your questions.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you subscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent fraud.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share only what is needed with trusted service providers — payment processors, shipping carriers and email providers — who are required to protect it.

Cookies & local storage

This site stores your bag contents in your own browser (local storage) so they're remembered between pages. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Residents of California (CCPA) and the EU/UK (GDPR) have additional rights, which we honor for all customers.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@thewellnessedit.com or use our contact form.

This is a school project; no real personal data is collected by this demo site.
