About us

Wellness should feel less like a project and more like a practice.

The Wellness Edit began with a simple idea: two honest formulas, clearly labelled, made to fit real life. No overflowing shelves. No loud promises.

Our story

Rooted in nature. Guided by science.

We started The Wellness Edit because the supplement aisle felt overwhelming. Hundreds of bottles, confusing labels, big claims. We wanted the opposite: a small, intentional collection that respects your time and your intelligence.

Our first two formulas use botanicals with long traditions: ashwagandha from India's Ayurvedic practice, and moringa, valued across South Asia, Africa and Latin America, presented with clear names and clearly stated amounts.

Our approach

Nature sets the pace.
Clarity leads the way.

Purpose over excess.

Every ingredient has a place. We would rather make two things well than twenty things loudly.

Information over hype.

Understand what you take. Our labels and ingredient notes are written to be read, not skimmed.

Ritual over reinvention.

Start where you are. Consistency matters more than a perfect routine.
