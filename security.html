Security

Shop with peace of mind.

Protecting your information is part of caring for you. Here is how we keep every visit and every order secure.

Encrypted connection

The whole site is served over HTTPS with TLS encryption, so information sent between your browser and us can't be read by others. Look for the padlock in your address bar.

PCI-compliant payments

Payments are handled by a PCI DSS Level 1 certified processor. Card numbers never touch our servers and are never stored by us.

Fraud monitoring

Orders are screened for unusual activity, and we will never ask for your password or full card number by email or phone.

Keeping yourself safe

  • Only shop at addresses that begin with https://.
  • Be wary of emails claiming to be from us that ask for payment details. Our emails come only from @thewellnessedit.com.
  • Keep your browser and device up to date.

Report a security issue

If you notice something suspicious on our site, please contact our webmaster, Diwash Adhikari, at webmaster@thewellnessedit.com.

Demo note: this school-project storefront does not take real payments. No card data entered at checkout is sent or stored anywhere.
