Products

Which formula should I start with?
The Chill Pill contains ashwagandha; Daily Green contains moringa. Start with your diet and individual needs, not a promise. A healthcare professional can help you decide whether either formula is right for you.
Can I take The Chill Pill and Daily Green together?
Many people take Daily Green in the morning and The Chill Pill in the evening. Always follow each label and check with a healthcare professional first.
How do I take each supplement?
Follow the directions on each label. Daily Green fits best with breakfast; The Chill Pill with an evening meal or wind-down routine.
How many capsules are in each bottle?
The Chill Pill has 60 supplements per container. Daily Green has 180 supplements per container.
Are the capsules vegan?
Yes. Both formulas use plant-based (HPMC) capsules.
